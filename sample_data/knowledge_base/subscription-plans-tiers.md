# Subscription Plans, Feature Comparison, and Quota Matrix

CloudDesk offers four tiers designed to scale with your organization's support volume.

## Feature Matrix

| Feature | Free | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Base Price | $0 | $29 | $99 | $299 |
| Seat Inclusion | 1 seat | Up to 10 seats | Up to 25 seats | Custom / Unlimited |
| Storage Quota | 10 GB | 50 GB | 200 GB | 1,000 GB+ |
| API Rate Limit | 60 rpm | 200 rpm | 500 rpm | 1,000–2,000 rpm |
| SAML / Okta SSO | No | No | Optional ($50) | Included |
| SLA Response Times | Best effort | 24 business hours | 4 business hours | 1 hour 24/7 |
| Audit Log Retention | 7 days | 30 days | 90 days | 365 days / Immutable |
