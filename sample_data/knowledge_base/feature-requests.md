# Submitting and Tracking Product Feature Requests

We continuously improve CloudDesk based on direct customer feedback and engineering requirements.

## Triage and Severity Classification
All tickets categorized as `feature_request` (such as dark mode requests, custom chart themes, or new integration connectors) are automatically assigned **Low severity** by system policy. They do not block operations or trigger on-call engineering alerts.

## Product Review Process
1. Feature suggestions are linked to our internal Product Roadmap board.
2. The product engineering team reviews votes and demand trends during monthly sprint planning cycles.
3. Customers are automatically notified via email when requested features enter public beta or general availability.
