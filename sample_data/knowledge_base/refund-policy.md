# Refund Policy and Seat Adjustment Guidelines

CloudDesk provides transparent and fair billing practices for all subscription tiers.

## Seat Reductions and Proration

When an organization removes team members or down-sizes seat counts:
1. **Billing Cycle Adjustment**: Removed seats are credited on a prorated basis for the remaining days in the active billing cycle.
2. **Auto-Refund Threshold**: Refunds under **$100.00 USD** can be automatically processed by the billing agent service without human authorization.
3. **Approval Gate Requirement**: Any refund or credit exceeding **$100.00 USD** strictly requires manual review and sign-off by a Human Support Lead via `POST /approvals/{id}/approve`.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Custom domain setup fees and one-time professional onboarding services are non-refundable.
- Partial months for voluntarily canceled enterprise contracts prior to commitment terms are subject to terms outlined in the Master Services Agreement (MSA).
