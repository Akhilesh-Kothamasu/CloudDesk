# Account Deletion and GDPR Right to Erasure Procedures

CloudDesk strictly complies with data privacy regulations, including GDPR Article 17 and CCPA deletion standards.

## Mandatory Privacy Escalation
All requests for permanent organization deletion, workspace teardown, or PII erasure must be routed to the Data Privacy Officer (DPO) and Legal Compliance team. AI agents cannot perform permanent database destruction without verified written consent and legal identity validation.

## Process Overview
1. **Identity Verification**: The DPO team verifies that the requesting party is the designated Legal Signatory or Primary Account Owner.
2. **Export Period**: A 14-day grace period allows workspace owners to download archival backups of their tickets, attachments, and audit logs.
3. **Hard Deletion**: Once approved, all cryptographic keys, databases, backups, and storage buckets associated with the workspace are permanently destroyed across all cloud regions.
